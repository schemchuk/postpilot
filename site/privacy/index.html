Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Who uses postpilot

postpilot is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Volodymyr Shemchuk, who connects his own social media accounts. No other person can sign in.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization postpilot uploads his own videos, their titles, descriptions and thumbnails to his YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services (scope youtube.upload), uploads his own videos to his TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish), and publishes his posts to his LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It does not read other people's data, contacts or private messages.

How data is stored

Access tokens are kept encrypted in the project's database and in GitHub Secrets. Published post IDs and publication status are stored to avoid posting the same item twice. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google user data

postpilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok user data

From TikTok postpilot uses only the owner's basic profile (open ID, display name) and the status of his own uploads. See the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions page and in the settings of each social network. Revoked or deleted tokens are removed from postpilot.
